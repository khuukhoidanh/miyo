## Scope
`memories/index.html` — Experience. Primary audience: students visiting from a personal QR on mobile. Keep the shared trip timeline, profile personalization, navigation, and Vietnamese copy; replace the visual layout and reduce stock imagery.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is a monochrome film strip made from a few long pauses: the trip is remembered through scale, typography, and scroll rather than a wall of photos. Refuses the default collage/gallery grid.
OWN-WORLD: Pure black, paper white, and intermediate grays; hard full-width color fields, one-pixel rules, oversized grotesk chapter typography, compact sans body text. Photos are scarce, grayscale, and treated as full-bleed scene plates, never cards.
STORY: Begin inside one shared memory, move through the first meeting, Ha Long, and the final goodbye; offer a direct route to personal thanks when the QR identifies a student.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height grayscale photograph under a black overlay; MIYO navigation at top; oversized Vietnamese headline anchored low-left; a thin route line and direct “Đi qua hành trình” action complete the opening. On narrow screens the copy stays inside the hero and the next chapter begins immediately below it.
